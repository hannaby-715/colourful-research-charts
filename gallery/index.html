How the colours work

  • Ask for a chart and the skill picks a palette and a font at random. Yours will not look like your neighbour's, even on the same file.
  • The numbers never change. Only the colours and the font are random. That is the point of the exercise.
  • Want one you saw here? Name it: --palette twilight-gold or --font magazine.
  • Want the same colours twice? Add --seed 42.
Both pages were produced by the skill itself, on real data. Palette swatches are read straight out of the skill's own palettes.json, so this gallery always matches what the renderer actually uses.
