Eighteen palettes

Every palette the renderer can use, each shown as its own colour ramp and applied to the same real chart. Leaving --palette out draws one of these at random, and that is the default.

Force one: add --palette twilight-gold to any command. Repeat a draw: add --seed 42. List them in the terminal: render.py list.
